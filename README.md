
PRIVACY POLICY

Flood & Fortune does not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Saved progress
To let you continue a voyage, the game stores your progress (level, cash, cargo and hull) in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to a server. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

Fonts
The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your IP address and browser details, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking
The game does not set cookies and does not track you across sites.

Children
Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with an updated date.
